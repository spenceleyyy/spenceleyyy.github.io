Opening · Why leave the sandbox
When a research question outgrows the lab

The scientific question, not the grant, is the reason to cross the boundary.

INTEGRATION
The problem crosses disciplines
No single perspective can frame it.
INTERDEPENDENCE
The parts affect one another
Separate work packages won't answer it.
CONTEXT
The answer must work in context
Users and partners shape the research.
AND MEANWHILE
The landscape is changing
Rising complexity · Fast technological change · More cross-sector teams · Funder interest in integrated research

Exercise 4 · In pairs
Did the science fail, or did the team system fail first?

Take one blue note from Exercise 1. Walk your partner through four questions.

  1. IntendedWhat were we trying to do?
  2. HappenedWhat happened? Earliest warning sign?
  3. WhyWhich circle did it start in?
  4. Next timeWhat would we put in place earlier?
Failure from disciplined experimentation advances science. Failure from an avoidable team-system defect doesn't.

Direction

References · the evidence behind each exercise
Sandbox & warrant check

Seven features that make team science hard (diversity, deep knowledge integration, size, goal misalignment, permeable boundaries, geographic dispersion, task interdependence): NRC (2015). Shifts since 2015 in technology and team makeup: NASEM (2025).

Ex 1 · Stages

Development, conceptualization, implementation, translation: Hall et al. (2012), as used in NASEM (2025) Conclusion 3-1.

Ex 2 · Expert team

Deep knowledge integration and high task interdependence: NRC (2015). Coordination, information sharing, conflict management; cohesion, shared mental models, transactive memory: NASEM (2025) ch. 3.

Ex 3 · Roles

Team Role Experience and Orientation (TREO): Mathieu et al. (2015).

Ex 4 · Failure debrief

Regular debriefs after each stage: NASEM (2025) Conclusion 3-1, implementation stage.

Ex 5 · Charter

Team charters and deliberate planning: NASEM (2025) Conclusion 3-1. Team launch: Penn State CTSI Team Science Toolkit. Bennett, Gadlin & Marchand (2018).

Ex 6 · Barrier wall

Incentives that discourage team science: NASEM (2025) Conclusion 4-1. Credit in promotion and tenure: Rec. 4-1. Institutional resources for teams: Rec. 4-4.
