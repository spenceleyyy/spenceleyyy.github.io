Mentorship tracks

Choose the level of support that fits your bandwidth.

Launchpad

For first-time research assistants or students transitioning into human factors.

  • Weekly 30-minute sprints with annotated feedback
  • Baseline training in EEG/fNIRS setups and experimental design
  • Pairing with a senior mentor for accountability

Builder

For contributors already embedded in a project who need sharper execution.

  • Bi-weekly technical design reviews
  • Metrics dashboards and replication-ready scripts
  • Access to cross-lab working groups

Lead

For senior students, postdocs, or industry partners steering their own teams.

  • Quarterly strategy sessions with lab leadership
  • Grant/paper pre-submission audits
  • Delegation frameworks and hiring playbooks
Process

What happens when you sign up.

01

Kickoff intake

Share your goals, strengths, and blockers. We co-author a 90-day roadmap with measurable outcomes.

02

Working cadence

Lock in meeting rhythms, async review channels, and metrics dashboards tailored to your track.

03

Feedback loops

Ship work, get annotations within 48 hours, and iterate using our shared template library.

04

Showcase + next steps

Wrap up with a portfolio-ready artifact, recommendation notes, and an intentional next-stage plan.

Who is eligible?

Undergrads, graduate students, visiting researchers, and collaborators across partner labs or industry teams.

How do we meet?

Hybrid by default—mix of in-person lab sessions and remote working rooms with recorded recaps.

What is the time commitment?

Launchpad averages 2–3 hrs/week, Builder 3–5 hrs/week, and Lead engagements are scoped around deliverables.

Can I join mid-quarter?

Yes, we onboard on a rolling basis as long as there's capacity in your preferred track.
