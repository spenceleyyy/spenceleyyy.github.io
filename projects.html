FIPRED: Predictive Automated Displays for Wildfire Management

Robert J. Spenceley, Christopher D. Wickens, Bianca de Toledo Piza

Predictor displays are a form of automation shown to provide notable benefit in many environments and are applied to wildfire suppression in this study. Twenty-two participants predicted fire trajectories using an automated predictor plus raw data. Predictor benefit was evident across scenarios, with significant signs of automation bias when the automation erred.

When raw data influences were removed in a control study, performance dropped, highlighting the value of raw data in calibrating dependence on the predictor. The FIPRED display improved prediction accuracy, but overreliance revealed automation bias, underscoring the need for careful trust calibration in high-stakes contexts.
