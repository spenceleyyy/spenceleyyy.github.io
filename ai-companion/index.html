Rules in brief
  • Keep at least one colonist alive for 5 minutes.
  • Each colonist eats 2 food and draws 1 energy per 10 s. A staffed farm gives 8 food (and draws 1 energy). A staffed generator gives 8 energy. Unstaffed structures produce nothing.
  • Every structure costs 0.5 materials per 10 s. At zero materials for 10 s, something breaks.
  • Food at zero: a colonist starves every 4 s. Energy at zero: farms run at half.
  • Tasks need idle villagers and take time. More workers finish faster, up to 4. Cost is paid at the start; mining pays at the end and thins each run.
  • A colonist arrives every 20 s while there is room and food and energy hold. Dust storms halve generators for 10 s.
  • Keys: 1–4 start a task, f/F and p/P add/remove farmers and power crew, Space pauses.
