Neuroergonomics

Featured projects.

HRI

Physical Human-Robot Interaction

This project focused on understanding how human biomechanics and neural responses change when physically collaborating with a UR10e robotic arm, especially when the robot's reliability is varied. Experiments were conducted using a breadth of bioinstrumentation, including EEG, fNIRS, eye-tracking, AMTI Force Plates, EMG, and ECG. I utilized methods and software such as Lab Streaming Layer (LSL), Python, and MATLAB to streamline data collection and perform detailed data analysis across these methodologies.

NEURAL

Neurodynamics of taskwork and teamwork in multihuman-robot teams

This research investigates the **neurodynamics** of taskwork and teamwork in Search and Rescue (SAR) teams as they transition between multi-human and multi-human-robot configurations. Using **EEG and EEG hyperscanning** (interbrain synchrony), the study analyzed how the team's neural patterns changed when a human navigator was replaced by a virtual robot navigator. The project's goal was to show that multi-human robot teaming is a fundamentally distinct team construct that alters both taskwork and teamwork, regardless of the robot's capabilities.
